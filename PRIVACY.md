# MonoTracker Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026

MonoTracker is a private season and score tracker for small groups. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

## Information We Collect

MonoTracker stores information entered by people who have access to the app, including player names, season names and dates, game placements, guest-player names, scores, and custom house rules. This information is required to provide shared leaderboards, history, statistics, and real-time synchronization.

Cloud Firestore may also process limited technical information such as device type, operating system, app bundle identifier, and developer platform as part of the Firebase user agent used to operate the service.

## How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide app functionality, including synchronizing seasons and games, calculating rankings, showing statistics, and displaying shared rules. MonoTracker does not use information for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

## Sharing and Service Providers

MonoTracker uses Google Firebase Cloud Firestore to store and synchronize app data. Data is processed by Google as a service provider under the applicable Firebase and Google Cloud terms. We do not sell personal information or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

## Access and Retention

Information remains stored until it is edited or deleted through the app. People with the shared app password can view and manage the group's data. Do not enter sensitive personal information into player names, season names, or house rules.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Firebase. Access to the app is protected by a shared password. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## Children's Privacy

MonoTracker is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app or its service providers change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
